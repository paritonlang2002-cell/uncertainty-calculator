How uncertainty propagation works
• Addition/subtraction: ΔR = √(ΔA² + ΔB²)
• Multiplication/division: ΔR/|R| = √((ΔA/A)² + (ΔB/B)²)
• Power: ΔR = |n × Aⁿ⁻¹| × ΔA
• Square root: ΔR = ΔA / (2√A)

These formulas assume independent measurements and small uncertainties for the multiplication and division approximations. Uncertainties entered here are absolute uncertainties.
